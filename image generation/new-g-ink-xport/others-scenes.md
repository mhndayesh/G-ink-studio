## Chapter 1: The Vanishing Boss & The Smell of Burnt Reality

Chapter purpose: Introduce the first concrete anomaly that breaks Kinji's apathy and sets the mystery in motion.
Chapter summary: After a routine patrol, Kinji is approached by Odo, who reveals that local crime boss 'Iron' Takeda has vanished overnight. The police report it as a simple disappearance, but Kinji notices something wrong when he visits Takeda's abandoned office: the air shimmers with a sickly pale green distortion, and the shadows don't match the furniture. Hina joins them, bringing coffee and complaining about the unnatural quiet in the streets. As they investigate, Kinji's intuition flares—a natural anchor reacting to dimensional drift. They find Takeda's last cigarette still burning on a desk that shouldn't have been there seconds ago, proving reality is being rewritten around him.
Chapter conflict: Kinji vs. The Unexplained Anomaly (Takeda's disappearance and the localized reality fracture).
Chapter hook: A Veil Syndicate operative in an obsidian trench coat watches them from across the street, tapping a frequency-monitoring pin that pulses green before vanishing into thin air.
